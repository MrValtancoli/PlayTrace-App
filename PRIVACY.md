# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 20 September 2026
**Applies to:** PlayTrace for Android (package `it.zonacalciolab.playtrace`)

## Summary

PlayTrace does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has
no servers, no accounts and no network access. Everything you create stays on
your device until you choose to share it yourself.

## What the app stores

PlayTrace saves the following on your device only, using the operating
system's private application storage:

- Match information you enter: competition, date, venue, team names, half
  duration.
- Your tag configuration: names, colours and enabled state.
- The events you tag during a match, with their four time references.
- Your appearance preference (system, light or dark).

This data is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
- No network requests of any kind. The app works entirely offline.
- No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Sharing an export

When you export a match, PlayTrace writes a JSON or CSV file to your device's
temporary storage and hands it to the Android share sheet. You decide where it
goes — email, cloud storage, a messaging app, or nowhere at all.

Once you pass a file to another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.
PlayTrace has no visibility into, or control over, what happens next.

## Children

PlayTrace is a tool for match analysis and is not directed at children. It
collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access,
export, correct or delete on your behalf. You are in full control: the data
lives in the app, and removing the app removes it.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be
updated here and the change noted in the release notes for that version.
